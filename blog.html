DIGITAL SAFETY · 2 MIN READ

Before you click

Unexpected messages often create urgency. Stop and inspect the request rather than reacting immediately.

Open the official app or type the known address yourself. Report suspicious messages through the service or your workplace.


DIGITAL SAFETY · 2 MIN READ

A password reset for your habits

Use a password manager to create a unique password for every account. Start with email and financial accounts.

Enable MFA or passkeys where available. Never share verification codes with someone who contacts you.


DIGITAL SAFETY · 2 MIN READ

Make your phone safer

Install operating system updates and review permissions for apps you no longer use.

Use a screen lock and maintain backups. If your phone is lost, use the official device recovery service.
