LESSON 01

Know the threats.

Recognize common online threats before they catch you off guard.

01

Phishing

Fake messages and websites try to steal information. Watch for urgent bank alerts, delivery texts and password reset traps. Verify through a trusted channel.

02

Ransomware

Malware can encrypt files or block access. Keep offline or versioned backups, update devices and avoid unexpected attachments.

03

Social engineering

Scammers manipulate trust, fear or urgency. Pause before sharing information, sending money or granting remote access.

04

Malware

Malicious software may steal information or damage devices. Download apps from trusted sources and keep security tools updated.
