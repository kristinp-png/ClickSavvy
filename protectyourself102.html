LESSON 02

Build your digital armor.

Practical habits for protecting your devices, accounts and identity.

01

Unique passwords

Use long, unique passwords or passphrases. A password manager helps generate and store them.

02

Multifactor authentication

Enable MFA. Prefer passkeys or security keys where supported; authenticator apps are another option.

03

Update automatically

Keep your operating system, browser and apps updated.

04

Safer connections

Use HTTPS and trusted networks. Avoid sensitive activity on suspicious Wi-Fi; a VPN does not make a fraudulent site safe.

05

Back up your files

Keep separate backups and test that you can restore them.

06

Privacy settings

Review app permissions, limit public personal information and remove unused accounts.
